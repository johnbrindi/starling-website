Can't connect, or asked to log in again and again?

Turn off the private / random Wi-Fi address for this network, then reconnect:

  1. Android: Settings → Wi-Fi → tap the gear next to Starling Wi-Fi → Privacy (MAC address type) → Use device MAC.
  2. iPhone: Settings → Wi-Fi → tap ⓘ next to Starling Wi-Fi → turn off Private Wi-Fi Address.
  3. Type your code or username without spaces. Usernames are lower-case.

Your time counts down from your first login. You are logged out automatically when it ends.
